EWrite a question and a short answer/5

כתבו שאלה עם Do / Does ותשובה קצרה לפי הסימן: ✔ = Yes, ✘ = No.

  1. Wolves eat people. ✘Do wolves eat people? No, they don't.
  2. Peter listens to his grandfather. ✘Does Peter listen to his grandfather? No, he doesn't.
  3. Fairy tales have heroes. ✔Do fairy tales have heroes? Yes, they do.
  4. Your sister reads every day. ✔Does your sister read every day? Yes, she does.
  5. A wolf sleeps at night. ✘Does a wolf sleep at night? No, it doesn't.
